## The entry

> For six winters her father watched from the porch as Ana slipped on the same icy step, until tonight, through falling snow, she carried two buckets of coal up it without stopping, set them down, and looked back at him smiling.

A small person doing something hard, a long effort finally landing. Six winters of slipping on the same icy step, then one night she carries two buckets of coal up it without stopping. The pride is never named, congratulated, or explained — it arrives quietly in the last word.
